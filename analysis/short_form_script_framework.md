# MATRX Short-Form Script Framework

This system is for `Instagram Reels` and `TikTok`, not keynote-style product explainers.

## Target shape

- Runtime: `20s` to `45s`
- Best density window for MATRX: `26s` to `35s`
- Goal: stop the scroll with the words alone
- Focus: spoken phrasing, not sound design or edit tricks

## The structure we should default to

1. Hook
State the tension in the first line. No setup. No throat clearing.

2. Hidden tax
Explain what the audience is already paying for without naming it clearly: repeated context, repeated debugging, repeated mistakes, AI forgetting the thread.

3. Missing layer
Reframe the problem. The model is not the whole issue. The system around the model is missing.

4. Mechanism
Explain what MATRX does in plain English: memory, rules, context, outcomes, continuity.

5. Payoff
Close with the business implication: less rework, more consistency, governed AI, compounding quality, infrastructure instead of chat.

## What to steal from creators

### Alex Hormozi
- Best use: blunt contrarian hooks and “hidden reason” framing.
- Steal:
  - opening with the claim, not the setup
  - saying what the audience is doing wrong in one line
  - making the problem feel expensive immediately
- Do not steal:
  - over-aggressive sales tone
  - multi-claim clips

### Leila Hormozi
- Best use: operator pain and systems language.
- Steal:
  - turning people problems into systems problems
  - short, CEO-sounding truths
  - principle-based closes
- Do not steal:
  - long management exposition

### Codie Sanchez
- Best use: hidden-tax and overlooked-asset framing.
- Steal:
  - contrarian openings
  - “everyone is chasing X, missing Y” structure
  - ownership / leverage language
- Do not steal:
  - forcing every Matrix script into a money-twitter tone

### Simon Squibb
- Best use: human-first story versions of MATRX, especially founder pain or team pain.
- Steal:
  - empathy first
  - one clean reveal
  - simple language
- Do not steal:
  - turning product scripts into soft inspiration

### Gary Vaynerchuk
- Best use: making scripts sound native to social instead of ad-polished.
- Steal:
  - direct point of view
  - conversational cadence
  - one takeaway per clip
- Do not steal:
  - noisy rant energy when the product needs clarity

## Default MATRX creator blend

Use this blend first:

- `alex-hormozi`
- `leila-hormozi`
- `codie-sanchez`

Why:
- Alex gives the stop-scroll claim.
- Leila gives the operator/system framing.
- Codie gives the hidden-cost / leverage angle.

## Script rules for MATRX

- One idea per clip.
- Product name can wait until line two or three if the pain is stronger than the brand.
- Every sentence must survive as a caption.
- Prefer:
  - “The problem isn’t X. It’s Y.”
  - “The hidden cost is…”
  - “What teams actually need is…”
  - “This is the missing layer…”
- Avoid:
  - long scene setting
  - feature lists
  - generic “AI is changing everything” openings
  - trying to explain the whole architecture in one short
