## MATRX Angle
- Problem: Teams keep re-explaining the same context, re-litigating the same decisions, and re-debugging the same failures because the assistant never truly becomes part of the organization.
- Solution: MTRX is the intelligent layer that sits between you or your team and the AI coding assistants you already use, so those assistants can remember the right things, stay inside your org's rules and limits, and learn from what actually happened in your projects.
- Mechanism: MATRX surfaces that context at the right moment so the assistant's answers fit the real situation better without anyone re-pasting everything every time.
- Payoff: MTRX turns AI coding assistants into a managed company capability: governed, measurable, and grounded in what your teams actually build.
